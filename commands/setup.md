# /setup

Connect your documents, images, and email platform. Three questions. Every newsletter gets smarter after this.

---

**1. What's your Box folder for this brand?**

The folder where your brand guide, audience personas, past newsletters, and performance data live. Exact folder name or path.

*Example: "See Monterey" or "Clients/See Monterey/Marketing"*

---

**2. What's your Cloudinary folder or tag for newsletter images?**

The folder, tag, or search term that identifies your newsletter-ready images in Cloudinary. If you have a structured library, a folder path works best. If you tag images, a tag name works too.

*Example: "newsletter-assets" or "clients/see-monterey/images" or tag: "newsletter"*

---

**3. How many audience segments do you actively email in Beehiiv?**

This sizes the segment variants in each `/create` run. If you're not on Beehiiv yet, that's fine — the newsletter output formats for easy paste into any platform.

*Example: "3 — newsletter subscribers, past customers, leads" or "1 — everyone gets the same email"*

---

After you answer, I'll connect to your Box folder, scan your Cloudinary library, and show you a one-line summary of what I found — including how many images are available and whether learning files already exist.

If something important is missing, I'll tell you what it is and how it affects output — but I won't block you from creating newsletters.

---

**What works best:**

**Box folder structure:**
```
Your Brand Folder/
├── brand-guide.md         (or .pdf, .docx — any format)
├── audience-personas.md
├── past-newsletters/      ← examples of what you've sent before
├── performance/           ← open rates, click rates, any data you have
├── newsletter-log.md      ← written by /track
├── newsletter-learnings.md
└── newsletter-baselines.md
```

**Cloudinary structure:**
```
Your image library — organized however works for you:
- By client folder
- By tag (newsletter, product, lifestyle, headshot)
- By campaign
```

You don't need all of these. A brand guide in Box and a few images in Cloudinary is enough to start.

---

*This is optional. `/create` works without setup — you can paste brand context, describe your audience, or provide examples directly. Setup just means you don't have to repeat yourself every session.*
